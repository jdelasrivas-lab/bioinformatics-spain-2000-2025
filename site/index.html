03

Evaluate the grouping

Compare separation and reproducibility across clustering solutions.

5 random seeds per alternative

Leiden at resolution 0.5 achieves the highest mean global silhouette among the supplied solutions (0.16820). However, this low positive value indicates weak separation and substantial thematic overlap in the original embedding space; it does not demonstrate clearly distinct research groups. K-means with five groups shows greater stability across seeds (mean ARI 0.988). The overlap may partly reflect the interdisciplinary nature of bioinformatics, where methods, data types and biological questions are shared across research areas. This is a possible interpretation rather than an established explanation; embedding and clustering choices may also influence separation. The proposed hierarchy is therefore an exploratory framework that combines quantitative evidence with the interpretation of terms and example articles.

Silhouette describes separation in the original embedding space. Values near zero indicate overlap; this descriptive score is not a p-value.
Adjusted Rand index (ARI) measures agreement between seeds. It does not provide external validation.

04

About the analysis

How to read the map and interpret the evidence.

01 / Article representations

BiomedBERT embeddings

Representations were extracted from the supplied BiomedBERT checkpoint. Non-special token vectors were averaged per fragment and L2-normalised; fragment vectors were then averaged and normalised per article. The encoder was frozen during extraction.

02 / Shared coordinates

One map, multiple solutions

All 22 clustering solutions use the same supplied UMAP coordinates. Changing a solution, label or colour only changes the display. The 5,785 mapped records are a subset of the full 5,914-publication corpus; this viewer does not assign a reason to the difference.

03 / Interpretation

An exploratory hierarchy

The representative Leiden solution contains five broad groups. Four are subdivided into 15 proposed local subtopics; group 5 remains undivided. Topic names are working interpretations. UMAP distances and visual overlap alone do not establish cluster quality or scientific boundaries.

Technical provenance and analysis configuration

The checkpoint identifier is retained from the supplied analysis. Its name indicates a fine-tuned checkpoint; frozen extraction does not imply that it was never fine-tuned. Absolute workstation paths have been reduced to their filenames for this public viewer.
